How Meltwater defines sentiment

Meltwater uses natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning to analyze each article at the sentence level, then combines sentence scores into an overall document score. Each article is assigned one of three labels:

  • Neutral — The article is factual or informational in tone. It reports on events, data, or statements without expressing a clearly favorable or unfavorable opinion about the subject. Most news coverage falls here.
  • Positive — The language expresses a favorable attitude toward the subject — for example, editorial endorsements, supportive opinion pieces, or coverage that highlights benefits and progress.
  • Negative — The language expresses an unfavorable attitude toward the subject — for example, criticism, opposition, or coverage that highlights problems, failures, or controversy.

Sentiment is assigned automatically by Meltwater's AI and may not capture nuance in every article. Individual scores can be overridden in the platform; overrides are fed back into Meltwater's models to improve accuracy over time.
